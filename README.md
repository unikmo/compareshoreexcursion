# Compare Shore Excursions

Compare Shore Excursions is a cruise-focused price, value, and protection comparison product.

It helps cruise passengers compare official cruise-line shore excursions against independent alternatives from providers such as Viator, GetYourGuide, Shore Excursions Group, CruiseCompete, and eventually selected local operators.

The product does not own inventory, process bookings, operate tours, hold customer funds, or act as a tour operator. Users complete bookings directly with third-party providers through affiliate or referral links.

Core promise:

> Compare the ship’s excursion price with trusted independent options before you book — and understand the protection trade-off.

---

## Product Positioning

Compare Shore Excursions is a neutral cruiser-first comparison layer.

It exists because cruise passengers repeatedly face the same decision:

- book the official cruise-line excursion, usually at a higher price but with the ship-backed safety net
- book independently, often for less, but with more responsibility for timing and return-to-ship risk

Most individual providers only show their own inventory. Viator promotes Viator. GetYourGuide promotes GetYourGuide. Cruise lines promote cruise-line excursions. Compare Shore Excursions sits above these providers and puts the options side by side.

The product should answer:

> What does the cruise line charge, what do independent providers charge, what changes between the options, and is the saving worth the trade-off?

---

## Core User Problem

Cruise-line shore excursions are often significantly more expensive than comparable independent alternatives.

Cruisers know this, but the decision is not only about price. It is also about trust, timing, pickup location, cancellation terms, return-to-ship anxiety, reviews, and whether the independent option is truly comparable.

Compare Shore Excursions helps users see:

- the cruise-line official price
- independent provider prices
- estimated savings
- what is included
- what is missing
- pickup or meeting point differences
- duration differences
- return-to-ship risk
- cancellation flexibility
- review and quality signals
- contextual travel protection options where relevant

The product should not simply say:

> This is cheaper.

It should say:

> This is cheaper, here is what changes, and here is how to think about protection.

---

## Value + Protection

This product should lead with both value and protection.

Cruise passengers are not only asking:

> Can I save money?

They are also asking:

> What risk am I taking if I book outside the cruise line?

That is the core product advantage.

Every independent option should help the user understand:

- estimated saving
- pickup or meeting-point difference
- timing buffer before ship departure
- cancellation policy
- provider rating and review count
- whether return-to-ship protection is mentioned
- whether trip delay or interruption protection may be worth considering

The goal is not to scare users into insurance.

The goal is to help them make a better port-day decision:

> Save money where it makes sense, and understand how to protect the decision.

## What Compare Shore Excursions Is Not

Compare Shore Excursions is not:

- a cruise line
- a tour operator
- a travel agency
- a booking platform
- a payment processor
- a transport operator

The product should stay affiliate/referral-first to avoid unnecessary operational and regulatory complexity.

## Core Use Cases

### 1. Compare Official vs Independent Excursions

For users considering a cruise-line excursion but wanting to know whether they are overpaying.

Example flow:

1. User selects cruise port.
2. User enters cruise date or sailing date.
3. User selects activity type.
4. Product shows:
   - cruise-line official excursion
   - Viator alternatives
   - GetYourGuide alternatives
   - Shore Excursions Group alternatives
   - CruiseCompete or other referral options
   - selected local operators later
5. Product explains:
   - price difference
   - duration difference
   - pickup or meeting point
   - cancellation policy
   - reviews
   - return-to-ship considerations
6. User clicks out to book with the selected provider.

Example result:

- Cruise line official tour: €150
- Independent similar tour: €98
- Estimated saving: €52 per person
- Trade-off: independent meeting point, no cruise-line return guarantee
- Verdict: Similar tour, lower price

### 2. Compare Similar Activities in the Same Port

For users who know the port but not the exact tour.

Example: user is visiting Cozumel and wants a snorkeling excursion.

Compare Shore Excursions should show:

- cruise-line snorkeling option
- Viator snorkeling tours
- GetYourGuide snorkeling tours
- Shore Excursions Group snorkeling tours
- beach club packages
- catamaran tours
- private group options

The product should classify whether options are:

- same activity
- similar activity
- better value
- shorter but cheaper
- longer but more expensive
- private option that becomes attractive if split by a group

### 3. Risk-Aware Independent Booking

For users considering an independent tour.

The product should explain the key cruise-specific trade-off:

- official cruise-line excursions usually carry more ship-backed timing protection
- independent tours may save money but require the passenger to manage timing risk

The product should not scare users. It should help them make a clear decision.

Example copy:

> Independent tours can be much cheaper, but the ship may not wait if you return late. Check the timing buffer, pickup point, cancellation policy, and whether travel protection makes sense.

### 4. Contextual Protection Referral

Protection should appear contextually when a user chooses or seriously considers an independent excursion instead of the ship-backed option.

Example moment:

> You are saving €52 per person by booking outside the cruise line. Consider whether trip delay or interruption protection makes sense before giving up the ship excursion safety net.

Guardrail:

- link out to licensed insurance providers or aggregators
- do not quote policies
- do not bind coverage
- do not recommend a specific policy as advice
- do not act as an insurance seller

### 5. Group Matching Later

Group matching is Phase 2, not MVP.

The idea: cruise passengers on the same sailing may want to split a private tour, private driver, or small-group excursion.

Later, the product can allow users to:

- join a sailing-date waitlist
- see others comparing the same port activity
- split a private tour
- coordinate around a specific excursion
- reduce the cost of private options

This should not be built before comparison traffic is proven.

The comparison product must be useful to a single visitor with no other users present.

## Core Comparison Verdicts

Use clear verdict labels.

### Same Port Day. Lower Price.

Use when the independent option is clearly similar and cheaper.

### Similar Tour. Better Value.

Use when the independent option is not identical but appears stronger on value.

### Official Excursion Has the Safety Edge.

Use when the ship option is more expensive but meaningfully safer or more convenient.

### Independent Option Looks Strong.

Use when the independent option has strong reviews, good timing, convenient pickup, and meaningful savings.

### Not a Clean Match.

Use when the cheaper option is too different.

### Split a Private Tour.

Use later when group matching exists.

## Data Sources

### Viator

Viator is the first integration path.

Use affiliate/API access where available to retrieve excursion content, pricing, images, reviews, duration, and booking links.

Bookings should redirect to Viator.

The product should not process transactions.

### GetYourGuide

Use affiliate network access first.

Possible routes:

- Awin
- TradeDoubler
- Travelpayouts
- other approved affiliate feeds

### Shore Excursions Group

Investigate affiliate or referral partnership terms.

This provider is highly relevant because it is cruise-specific.

### CruiseCompete

Investigate referral possibilities.

Use as an additional comparison source where available.

### Cruise Line Official Excursion Prices

This is the hardest data source.

Cruise lines usually do not expose clean public pricing APIs for official excursion inventory.

For MVP, collect official cruise-line prices manually or semi-manually for selected ports and high-intent activities.

This can be refreshed weekly or periodically rather than in real time.

Do not assume full automation from day one.
